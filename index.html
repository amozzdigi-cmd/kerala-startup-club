Why Kerala Startup Club?

Kerala Startup Club is building a strong founder network where entrepreneurs, creators and startups can connect, collaborate, learn and grow together. We believe Kerala's next great companies are built by the people in this room.

Learning Sessions
Workshops and talks from founders who have built successful startups inside and outside Kerala.
